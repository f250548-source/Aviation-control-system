MayDay-404 - Air Traffic Control Simulation
University Data Structures Course Project

You are the Chief Radar Officer (CRO).
Manage corridors, emergencies, missing flights and credibility
across a 30-tick shift. Every wrong call costs the tower.

Modules: Registration, Hash Table, Queues/Heaps, Undo Stack,
BST/AVL, Graphs, BFS/DFS, Dijkstra, Kruskal MST, Game Engine.
